# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

![Stranger Things](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/38/Stranger_Things_logo.png)

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror television series created by the Duffer Brothers. It follows a group of friends who encounter supernatural mysteries, secret government experiments, and a terrifying alternate dimension called the *Upside Down*.

## Why I Like It
I like this series because it combines:
- Mystery and suspense
- Science fiction and supernatural elements
- Friendship, teamwork, and loyalty
- Interesting characters and unexpected plot twists

## My Favorite Characters
1. **Eleven** – A girl with extraordinary supernatural abilities.
2. **Mike Wheeler** – A loyal friend who cares deeply about his friends.
3. **Dustin Henderson** – The funny and intelligent member of the group.
4. **Steve Harrington** – A brave character who becomes a great friend and protector.

## Favorite Quote
